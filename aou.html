Avengers: Age of Ultron

Ranking: First Place
Rating: 4/5

Now THIS is family. I love Age of Ultron because you can really watch the Avengers just be a team. They aren't forming, they aren't breaking up (though they still argue a ton), they are just a team. We get to see them celebrate successful missions with parties! We get to see them laugh!
Beyond my enjoyment of a domestic superhero team, so much of the Marvel Cinematic Universe is rooted in this film! We would not have Infinity War, Civil War, WandaVision, or plenty of other important films without it. Even Black Panther's Wakanda teased in this film!
